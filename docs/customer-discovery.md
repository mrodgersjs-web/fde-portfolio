# Customer discovery

Questions that turn vague executive asks into buildable scope:

1. What decision or handoff is broken today?
2. Who owns the outcome after go-live?
3. What systems of record must remain authoritative?
4. What data classes are forbidden in model context?
5. Where must a human approve before action leaves the building?
6. What does "done" mean in a command you can re-run?
7. What is the rollback if the agent is wrong?

Output: scope boundary, risk tier, eval seeds, non-goals.

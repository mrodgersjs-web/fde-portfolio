## Operating principles

1. Outcome first — models are secondary to the customer workflow.
2. Ground every claim — source, constraint, or confidence bound.
3. Evals before scale — if it cannot be tested, it cannot be trusted.
4. Human approval where risk is real — autonomy is earned.
5. Auditability is product quality.
6. Ship the smallest useful loop, then harden it.
